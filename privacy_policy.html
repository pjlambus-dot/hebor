PRIVACY POLICY

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, such as submitting an inquiry through our Contact Us page or initiating a purchase, we may collect:

  • Full name and contact details (email address).
  • Order specifications (t-shirt name, size, quantity).
  • Messages, inquiries, or notes sent through our communication channels.

2. How We Use Your Information & Email Transparency

All inquiries, orders, and messages are securely transmitted via email. To ensure total transparency and accuracy:

  • We use your details exclusively to process your orders, answer your inquiries, and coordinate logistics.
  • Full Email Copies: A complete record or copy of your conversation and transaction details will be sent via email so that both you and our team have a transparent record of your requests.

3. Data Confidentiality

Hebor strictly safeguards your personal data. We do not sell, trade, or rent your private information to third parties. Your data is used solely for internal fulfillment and direct communication between you and our team.
