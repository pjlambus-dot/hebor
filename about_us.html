ABOUT US

HISTORY

Hebor, a local term in Hagonoy, Bulacan which means to make way to see someone you admire, whether it be through a chore, a simple gathering or an errand given to an individual. The founding group incorporated the term as a hint of local touch to the brand, and a testament on how whenever you feel the need for something new, you’re just a click away.

This modern take on a traditional Filipino cultural example creates a brand new taste for a fashion industry that innovates quickly around trends, preferences, and aesthetics. Hebor aims to provide quality, and authentic Filipino service that shoppers and customers would love to incorporate to their everyday lives especially fashion.
